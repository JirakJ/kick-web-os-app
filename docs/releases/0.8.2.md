The unofficial Kick TV app for LG webOS now fits home and replay browsing to the screen:

- Compact channel entry above three saved-channel panels. Their page size follows the available height and text size, with a visible footer and no vertical scrolling.
- Replay cards use the available thumbnail space and adapt their page size. Titles, dates and paging controls stay fully visible.
- Directional navigation follows the painted rows and columns. Resizing preserves the selected item or restores focus when a pager disappears.
- Long unavailable-status labels retain room for favorite channel names.

Verified on LG 55NANO863NA / webOS 5.6.2 / Chromium 68, with 720p/1080p viewport and text/contrast combinations, real replay 1080p/60, decoder cleanup and bounded resize stress. All 1,144 browser screens across 13 locales passed. Physical handset, spoken Voice Guide, audible output and extended-soak limits remain in the [device audit](https://github.com/JirakJ/kick-web-os-app/blob/main/docs/production-audit.md).

New native TV captures are attached below and to the documentation. SHA256SUMS covers the IPK and every attached PNG. Saved-channel lists are demonstration choices; replay catalog and playback use public content. Install with webOS Dev Manager or the existing Homebrew setup; unchanged app/service IDs preserve local data. Keep v0.8.1 for rollback.
