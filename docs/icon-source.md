# Launcher icon

The launcher uses Kick's green K mark from its public
[Apple touch icon](https://kick.com/apple-icon.png?apple-icon.0ldhg5ovdrppx.png),
retrieved on 12 September 2026. The artwork is resized to the webOS-required
80×80 (`app/icon.png`) and 130×130 (`app/largeIcon.png`) PNG sizes.

Kick retains rights to its brand artwork. The project remains an unofficial
player and does not imply endorsement by Kick or LG.
